## 2. Short Description – Play Store (max. 80 chars)

The short description is the teaser above the "read more" fold and the single biggest
conversion lever in the listing. It should carry benefit + differentiation in one line.

**Velm positioning:** notes + checklists + categories + reminders, plus the standout
feature "atomic thoughts → threads" (synthesis). Editorial, calm, no feature clutter.

### Variants (char count in brackets)

| # | Text | Chars | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Notes, checklists & thoughts — calmly organized, synced everywhere. | 67 | Breadth + sync |
| B | Just write it down. Velm weaves your thoughts into clear threads. | 65 | Differentiation (synthesis) |
| C | The notes app that thinks with you: scattered thoughts become threads. | 70 | Differentiation + benefit |
| D | Notes, lists & reminders — beautifully simple, always in sync. | 62 | Breadth + aesthetics |
| E | Capture your thoughts. Velm connects the ones that belong together. | 67 | Synthesis, narrative |

> **Recommendation:** variant **C** or **B** — they lead with the unique selling point
> (thought synthesis) instead of competing as "just another notes app".
> _Final choice: ⬜ still open_

feature/ASO-first (more keywords, scannable). Both use only features that actually exist

## 4. ASO Keywords

**Important:** the Play Store has **no** keyword field — ranking pulls terms from
**title + short description + full description**. ASO for Play = placing these keywords
naturally in those texts (§2/§3). The dedicated **100-char keyword field** only exists in
the **App Store (iOS)** — a ready draft is below.

_Status: 🟡 – iOS field drafted, Play integration pending final text_

### Keyword pool (prioritized)

**Primary (high volume, core):**
notes · note app · checklist · to-do · tasks · reminders · list · shopping list

**Secondary (function / intent):**
thoughts · ideas · brainstorm · notebook · memo · planner · organize · sync ·
categories · voice note

**Differentiation (Velm-specific, lower volume):**
connect thoughts · threads · ai notes · zettelkasten · second brain

**Deliberately omitted:** "velm" (it's in the title → redundant in the iOS field),
brand/competitor names (store policy).

### App Store – keyword field (iOS, max. 100 chars)

> Rules: no space after comma (saves chars), don't repeat words from the title,
> singular/plural partially covered automatically.

```
note,notes,checklist,todo,tasks,reminder,list,shopping,ideas,thoughts,planner,memo,organize
```
_(91 chars — verify in App Store Connect before submitting. "brainstorm/notebook/voice"
go into the Play full text instead.)_

### Play Store – keyword integration

Make sure these terms appear naturally in the short/full text (ideally primary keywords
2–3× in the full text, without keyword stuffing):

- [ ] "Notes" — near the title / first sentence
- [ ] "Checklist(s)" + "to-do"/"tasks"
- [ ] "Reminders"
- [ ] "Shopping list"
- [ ] "Categories" + "search/filter"
- [ ] "Thoughts" + "ideas" (differentiation)
- [ ] "sync / device sync"
- [ ] "voice note / dictate"

## 5. Screenshot Story (Play Store – Phone)

**Format:** Play Store Phone. Requirements: PNG/JPG, 16:9 or 9:16, min. 320 px,
max. 3840 px edge; 2–8 images. The **first 2–3 screenshots** decide (above the fold in
the listing) → most important message first.

**Narrative logic:** from familiar to special. Velm looks like a beautiful notes app
(hook via aesthetics + clarity), THEN comes the unique selling point (thoughts →
threads). This way we don't lose users who are "just" looking for a notes app, and we
surprise them with the extra value.

Each screenshot = one app screen with a short caption headline on top (marketing copy,
not part of the app UI). Keep captions short (flip-book friendly).

| # | Screen | Caption (suggestion) | What must be visible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (hero) | HomeScreen (notes list) | **"All your thoughts. One calm place."** | Well-filled, beautiful notes list: mixed categories (colored accents), 1 pinned note on top, 1 note with checklist preview, 1 with reminder icon. The editorial look must land. |
| 2 | EditorScreen | **"Write, check off, get reminded."** | A note being edited with a checklist (some items checked), category chip, reminder set. Shows core features at a glance. |
| 3 | ThreadsScreen | **"Velm connects what belongs together."** | Several threads with meaningful titles + summaries + thought count. THE differentiating feature. |
| 4 | ThreadDetailScreen | **"Loose notes become a single thread."** | One thread opened: summary + the linked notes below. Shows the "aha" moment of synthesis. |
| 5 | HomeScreen with filter/search active | **"Find anything, instantly."** | FilterBar active (category selected) or search field with hits. Shows organization/scalability. |
| 6 | QuickCapture / VoiceCaptureSheet | **"Catch it before it's gone."** | Quick-capture FAB open or voice sheet active. Shows fast capture + voice input. |

**Optional 7/8 (if space / A/B test):** SettingsAbo (plan overview, "Free is plenty") or
ArchiveScreen. Better to drop if the 6 are strong.

**Order note:** if an A/B test is desired — try a variant with the **thread screen as #1**
(differentiation immediately). The default above deliberately starts familiar.
